Siple AppImage: the runtime and what it contains
================================================

Siple's AppImage is one file: the AppImage type 2 runtime followed by a squashfs image of Siple.
This notice covers the runtime, which is not Siple's own code. Siple itself is FSL-1.1-ALv2
(see LICENSE); the licences of its Rust dependencies are in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.html.
The AppImage also bundles GUI libraries (X11, xkbcommon, Wayland): they are listed with their Debian
packages and versions in usr/share/doc/siple/BUNDLED-LIBRARIES.txt, and their licences are in
usr/share/doc/siple/bundled-licenses/.

The runtime is an unmodified upstream binary. The LGPL-2.1 libfuse is linked statically into it; the
exact sources and the patch it was built with are at the addresses above. You may rebuild the runtime
from them (scripts/docker/ and scripts/build-runtime.sh in the runtime's repository) and use the
result in place of the one in this file: copy the squashfs image that follows the runtime (it starts
at the offset printed by `<file> --appimage-offset`) behind your own runtime build.
